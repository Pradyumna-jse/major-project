How it works

Eight stages from registering a case to alerting the family.

1 Register
Officer adds details and photos.
2 Detect
YOLOv8 finds faces ({{detector}} active).
3 Embed
InsightFace turns each face into 512 numbers.
4 Match
Cosine similarity, Top-K, threshold.
5 Verify
An officer confirms or rejects.
6 Record
Time, place, score saved in SQLite.
7 Alert
E-mail to family and authority.
8 Map
Sightings shown on a Folium map.

Built-in safeguards

  • Face signatures are encrypted in the database.
  • Matches below the threshold are rejected, so someone is not named just because they are “closest”.
  • A human must confirm before any alert.
  • Video faces are tracked, so one person gives one candidate, not hundreds.
  • Every decision is written to an audit log.

Good to know

  • Clear, front-facing registration photos give the best results.
  • Poor light, masks or very small faces lower the score.
  • Never act on a match alone – always verify.
